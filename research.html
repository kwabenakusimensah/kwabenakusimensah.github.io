Teacher-Delivered Group CBT for Adolescent Depression in West Africa

Wellcome Trust · Ghana Country Site Co-PI

A multi-country consortium project to adapt, implement, and evaluate the effectiveness, acceptability, appropriateness, sustainability, and cost-effectiveness of a teacher-delivered, group-based cognitive behavioural therapy programme for depressive symptoms among adolescents across five West African countries, including Nigeria, Ghana, and Sierra Leone. The consortium has won a planning grant for the preliminary phase, with an invitation-only opportunity to bid for the five-year main implementation grant.

Genetic Architecture of Mood Disorders in African-Ancestry Populations

Mayo Clinic, with Drs Winham, Frye, Veldic & Biernacka

A two-part project. The first part, a scoping review mapping the genetic architecture and polygenic predictability of mood disorders in African-ancestry populations, is complete and its manuscript is currently in peer review. The second, ongoing part is a GWAS analysis using the All of Us dataset comparing the genetic architecture of mood disorders between African- and European-ancestry populations, alongside a Mendelian randomization analysis examining the causal relationship between obesity and sex and depression.

Ongoing

ACT Model — AI-Assisted Systematic Scoping Review

Mayo Clinic, with Dr Monica Taylor-Desir

A systematic scoping review of the efficacy, adaptability, and economic case for the Assertive Community Treatment (ACT) model for people with severe mental illness, with particular attention to implementation in low-resource settings. A methodological aim of the review is to evaluate the added value of pairing a contemporary AI systematic-review tool (Elicit) with a conventional, expert-led literature search — comparing a librarian-led database search against an AI semantic search for coverage, and using AI-assisted extraction and thematic synthesis under human verification.

High-Fat Diet, Depression & Cognitive Decline — Systematic Review

Choi Labs, Mayo Clinic, with Dr Doo-Sup Choi

A systematic review of the impact of high-fat diet on depression and cognitive decline; manuscript currently in peer review. A related follow-on analysis is underway on an ongoing animal-model study examining the neuroinflammatory impact of high-fat diet on depression-like behaviour and cognitive decline in AD-knockout mice at Choi Labs — the preliminary 24-week follow-up phase is complete, with the next phase about to commence.

Completed

Measurement Invariance of the WISC-IV Among Almajiri Children in Northern Nigeria

Applied Neuropsychology: Child, 2025

Assessed the factor structure and measurement invariance of the WISC-IV among almajiri children attending Quranic schools compared with pupils attending mainstream public primary schools in Northern Nigeria — part of a wider thread of work examining executive function and cognitive assessment among children excluded from mainstream evidence by geography, poverty, linguistic disadvantage, or marginalised schooling.

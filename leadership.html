Leadership & Advocacy

Professional Leadership

President, Psychiatric Association of Ghana (2025–2027)

Elected the 4th President of the PAG, a professional organisation of Ghanaian psychiatrists and member of the World Psychiatric Association. Previously served as Vice President (2021–2023).

Immediate Past President, African Association of Child and Adolescent Mental Health (AACAMH)

Served as President from December 2022 until handing over in July 2026, following service as General Secretary from 2018. AACAMH is an affiliate of IACAPAP working across 19 African countries to promote child and adolescent mental health on the continent.

Senior Research Advisor, NIHR East of England Research Design Service (2022–2023)

Advised applicants across the East of England — from senior medical academics to industry SMEs — on developing fundable research proposals for NIHR and similar funders.

Postgraduate Training Coordinator, KATH Dept. of Psychiatry & Secretary, WACP Faculty of Psychiatry (Ghana Chapter)

Coordinates residency and postgraduate psychiatric training at KATH. Recently promoted to Secretary of the WACP Faculty of Psychiatry (Ghana Chapter), having previously served as its Training Coordinator.

Founder & Patron, Heal the Children Foundation (2010–present)

Co-founded this charity as a medical student to help cover treatment costs for uninsured sick children at KATH; still active 15 years on, run by successive generations of medical students.

Patron, Nereus Support Network (2025–present)

A youth-led NGO supporting youth mental health through advocacy, education, and outreach — having reached 15,000 young people in Ghana to date.
